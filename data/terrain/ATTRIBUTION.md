# Terrain data attribution

`data/terrain/*/heights.i16` and `hillshade.png` are derived from **AWS Terrain Tiles** (Terrarium encoding, zoom 8, about
450 m a pixel), a public dataset at https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/ (tiles fetched from
`https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/Z/X/Y.png`, no key needed).

The tiles are blended by their publisher from these sources, each of which asks for credit:

- USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP) and the National Elevation Dataset: public domain (U.S. Geological Survey).
- NASA Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM) and USGS GMTED2010.
- NOAA ETOPO1.

Credit line to use in anything that shows this terrain: *Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (USGS 3DEP, SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1).*

The grids are regenerated by `tools/make_terrain.py` from a folder of downloaded tiles; the raw tiles are not kept in the
repository.

## Rivers

`data/rivers/*/rivers.json` is derived from **Natural Earth** 1:10m river centrelines (`ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines` and
`ne_10m_rivers_north_america`), https://www.naturalearthdata.com, which is in the public domain. No credit is required; a
courteous one: *Rivers: Natural Earth.* Regenerate with `tools/make_rivers.py`.

## Not stored in the repo

USGS 3D Elevation Program tiles (1 and 1/3 arc-second, public domain) were tested for later use at
`https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Elevation/1/TIFF/current/n45w104/USGS_1_n45w104.tif` (and `/13/` for 1/3 arc-second).
